You are an expert product storyteller, workshop designer, and front-end developer.                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
Build a self-contained, interactive single-page program (a narrative web app / digital "pitch experience") about the **Electronic Lab Notebook (ELN)** project.                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
## Purpose                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
1. **Inspire** functional teams (formulators, physicochemical lab, R&D, and other cross-functional teams) to co-build the ELN together.                                                                                                   
2. **Communicate the ideas and core value** of the project in a way that is clear, concrete, and motivating.                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
## Audience                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
Non-technical and semi-technical stakeholders from different functional teams who will both *use* and *contribute to* the ELN.                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
## Source ideas to convey (do not omit these)                                                                                                                                                                                             
1. The ELN must become a daily routine of everyone in R&D.                                                                                                                                                                                
2. It supports users in tracking the timeline of their experiments.                                                                                                                                                                       
3. It facilitates collaboration between formulators, physicochemical lab, and other functional teams.                                                                                                                                     
4. It ensures data integrity and acts as the single source of truth.                                                                                                                                                                      
5. It supports calculation tools for formulations.                                                                                                                                                                                        
6. It can track individual workload in R&D (present this honestly as an open, debatable topic for discussion).                                                                                                                            
7. It consists of four main groups of functions: **Overview**, **Library**, **Lab**, and **Analyze**.                                                                                                                                     
8. **Overview** focuses on the timeline of projects, risks, and individual workload.                                                                                                                                                      
9. **Library** contains pre-prepared information before lab work: ingredients, equipment, test methods, test panels, formulas.                                                                                                            
10. **Lab** records what actually happens: actual yield, samples from the batch, and test reports for each sample.                                                                                                                        
11. **Analyze** shows the analytical side: learnings from chains of DoEs and designing new DoEs.                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
## Core value proposition to emphasize                                                                                                                                                                                                    
- **One daily habit, one shared home for R&D work.**                                                                                                                                                                                      
- **Traceability:** every experiment has a clear, searchable timeline.                                                                                                                                                                    
- **Collaboration:** connects formulators, physicochemical lab, and other teams around shared data.                                                                                                                                       
- **Data integrity:** a single source of truth everyone can trust.                                                                                                                                                                        
- **Built-in intelligence:** formulation calculation tools and DoE-driven analysis.                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
## Deliverable requirements                                                                                                                                                                                                               
- A single, self-contained HTML program (HTML/CSS/JS in one file) that works offline.                                                                                                                                                     
- An engaging opening that states the vision and the "why now".                                                                                                                                                                           
- An interactive tour of the four modules (Overview, Library, Lab, Analyze), each with:                                                                                                                                                   
- a one-line purpose,                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
- a concrete example from daily lab work,                                                                                                                                                                                               
- and the value it brings to each functional team.                                                                                                                                                                                      
- A "core values" section (daily routine, traceability, collaboration, data integrity, calculation tools).                                                                                                                                
- A "how you can contribute" section with a clear call to action for each functional team.                                                                                                                                                
- A section that frames the "individual workload tracking" idea as an open question for teams to discuss, not a settled decision.                                                                                                         
- Clean, modern, visually appealing design with subtle animations; inspiring but grounded in practicality.                                                                                                                                
- Clear section navigation and a natural storytelling flow from "why" → "what" → "how we build it together".                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
## Tone                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
Inspiring, collaborative, practical, and honest. Avoid hype. Speak the language of scientists and lab teams.                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
## Success criteria                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
A reader should finish the program understanding:                                                                                                                                                                                         
- what the ELN is,                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
- the four modules and what each does,                                                                                                                                                                                                    
- why it matters to *their* team specifically,                                                                                                                                                                                            
- and a concrete way to get involved.
