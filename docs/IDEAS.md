# Ideas for Electronic Lab Notebook

1. Need to become a daily routine of everyone in R&D.
2. Support users to track the timeline of their experiments.
3. Facilitate the collaboration between formulators, physicochemical lab, and other functional teams.
4. Ensure data integrity to be the single source of truth.
5. Support calculation tools for formulations.
6. Tracking workload of each individuals in R&D (debatable).
7. Consist 4 main group of functions: (1) Overview (2) Library (3) Lab (4) Analyze
8. **Overview** focus on the timeline of projects, risks and individual workload.
9. **Library** contain the pre-prepared information before the actual lab work, such as ingredients, equipments, test methods, test panels, formulas.
10. **Lab** is where the actual things happened are recorded, such as actual yield, samples from the batch, and test reports for each sample.
11. **Analyze** show the analytical aspects of the lab, such as learnings from chains of DoEs, design new DoEs, etc.
